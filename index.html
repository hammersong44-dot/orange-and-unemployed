About Hungarian Vizslas

Hungarian Vizslas are an ancient breed, originating in Hungary centuries ago and documented in 10th-century Hungarian writings. We're known for our distinctive russet-gold coat, lean athletic build, and incredibly affectionate temperament. Vizslas are often called "Velcro dogs" because we love being close to our humans—we're loyal, energetic, and eager to please.

We're versatile hunters and athletes, but honestly? We just want to be your best friend. We thrive on companionship and are happiest when we're by your side, whether that's on an adventure or just hanging out at home.
